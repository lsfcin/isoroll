## Technique — occlusion & depth sorting

- **Last Asylum** — isometric game (Lucas, INBOX 2026-08-01) named as one that *"can teach us how to visualize iso scenes without too much z-blocking"*. Bears directly on **Phase 6** (painter's algorithm / cyclic occlusion) in `../ROADMAP.md`: the open question is which of its choices does the work — level layout that keeps tall occluders off camera-near cells, cutaway/fade-on-overlap for walls, or a slicing model like ours. Not yet examined; assessment task tracked in ROADMAP Backlog.
